# Security and limitations

- Product, labels and statements are supplied by the book owner. There is no supplier signature, source retrieval or physical verification. A consistent book may still contain false declarations.
- Hosted model interpretation is fallible. Validators can share model/provider failure modes. Address and vote diversity do not prove independently operated infrastructure. Consensus is not universal numerical or linguistic correctness.
- Ambiguous and conditional quantities are instructed to fail closed. Prompt injection is untrusted data, but the prompt and replay do not establish immunity. The local suite does not measure adversarial semantic accuracy.
- The book covers one fixed product and unconditional packaging-count equalities. It does not model mixed boxes, optional gifts, ranges, variant pack sizes, damaged goods, weights, prices, exchange rates or temporal changes.
- The first accepted consistent forest is authoritative within that book. Conflicts are retained and excluded, not resolved by choosing a supplier. There is no edit, deletion, override, correction or revocation. Consumers should inspect provenance and the entire attempt history; use a new book for corrections.
- Labels are not authenticated semantic types. Different labels can denote overlapping packaging concepts. Fixed product scope and explicit statements guide the model, but do not eliminate caller manipulation.
- Fractions are exact mathematical conversions. Whole units and remainders are disclosed; no physical divisibility, automatic rounding, delivery, custody or payment is promised.
- Quotas bound one namespace, not Sybil usage. Statements and addresses are public. Do not publish confidential supplier or personal information.
- Only successful finalized Normal/full-consensus receipts count as network evidence. IDLE votes are not successful semantic checks. Failed or uncertain transactions require inspection of the original hash before any separate action; tools do not perform Appeal.

Report issues through repository Issues. No public mainnet or security-audited production deployment is claimed.
